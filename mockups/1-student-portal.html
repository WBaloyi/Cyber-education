Suggestion 1

Student portal: "My learning"

A signed-in area where learners manage what they enrolled in.

Why add it
  • Learners can't see or change an enrollment after the confirmation message.
  • Cancelling frees the spot for someone else, so fewer quarters show "Full" when they aren't.
  • Simple build: a "magic link" email login, plus a progress column on the enrollments table.
