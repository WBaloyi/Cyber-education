Suggestion 2

Waitlist and start-date calendar

Turn "Full" into a waitlist, show all quarters at a glance, and help people remember their start date.

Why add it
  • A full quarter is a dead end today: the radio button is disabled.
  • Courses start on the 1st of every quarter, which fits a calendar view well.
  • Build: a waitlist table, and when someone cancels, offer the spot to the first person on the list. "Add to calendar" is a small .ics file.
