Fees & enrollment

{% call question('discounts', 'Are there any discounts for returning students?') %}

We love welcoming learners back. Discounts for returning students depend on the course, so send us a message with the course you're interested in and we'll tell you what's currently available.

{% endcall %} {% call question('enroll', 'How do I enroll, and are spaces limited?') %}
  1. Choose a course on the courses page and press Enroll.
  2. Pick the start date that suits you.
  3. Fill in your name and email to confirm your spot.

Each start date has a limited number of places, so we recommend enrolling early.

{% endcall %}

Certificates

{% call question('accreditation', 'Is this an accredited course?') %}

Every learner who completes a course receives a Cyber Education certificate of completion. If you need to know whether a course is formally accredited for a specific employer or institution, contact us and we'll confirm the details for that course.
